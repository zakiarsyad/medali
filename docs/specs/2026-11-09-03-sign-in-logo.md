# MEDALI logo on the sign-in page

## What we want

MEDALI gets its own logo: a medal with a ribbon and a star, in navy blue
and gold, standing for student character building at PKN STAN. The
sign-in page is redesigned around this logo so it feels welcoming and
shows what the app is for, in light and dark mode, on a computer and on a
phone.

## Who it is for

Everyone who signs in: students, mentors, dorm staff, counsellors, the unit
and admins.

## What should happen

- When anyone opens the sign-in page, then they see the MEDALI logo, the
  name "MEDALI", "PKN STAN" and the tagline "Membangun Karakter, Meraih
  MEDALI".
- When they look below the tagline, then they see six value words:
  Mandiri, Empatik, Disiplin, Adaptif, Loyal, berIntegritas, with the
  letters M, E, D, A, L, I marked so that together they spell MEDALI.
- When they press the Google sign-in button, then signing in works as it
  does today.
- When a sign-in is refused, then the reason still shows on the page.
- When the page is opened on a phone, then everything fits without
  scrolling sideways, and the button is easy to tap.
- When the device is in dark mode, then the logo and the page stay clear
  and easy to read.
- When the app is open in a browser tab, then the tab shows the small
  MEDALI logo.
- The "Kebijakan privasi" link stays on the page.

## Not included

- The official PKN STAN emblem. Only an original MEDALI logo is used, so no
  campus document or brand is copied.
- Changes to how sign-in works.
- The logo in the menu or on other pages.

## Open questions

None.
