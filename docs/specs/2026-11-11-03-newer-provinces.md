# Five newer provinces in Provinsi domisili

## What we want

The Provinsi domisili choices lack the five provinces formed since 2012.
Add Kalimantan Utara, Papua Barat Daya, Papua Pegunungan, Papua Selatan and
Papua Tengah, so a student from there can be entered correctly.

## Who it is for

Staff who add or change new students (Formulir, Ubah, Unggah berkas), and
anyone who filters by Provinsi.

## What should happen

1. When staff open the Provinsi domisili choice in Formulir or Ubah, then
   the five new provinces are there, in alphabetical order, with Luar negeri
   still last.
2. When staff save a student with one of the five, then the student detail
   and lists show that province.
3. When staff upload a file in Unggah berkas with one of the five in the
   Provinsi column, then the row is accepted.
4. When staff filter a list by Provinsi, then the five appear as choices,
   with how many students hold each.
5. Students already saved keep their province unchanged.

## Not included

- Moving existing students from Papua or Papua Barat to a new province.
- Changing any other list.

## Open questions

None.

## Steps

- [x] 1. Add the five provinces to the Provinsi domisili choices, in
  alphabetical order, with a check that they are accepted.
