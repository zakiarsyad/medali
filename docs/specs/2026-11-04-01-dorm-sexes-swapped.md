# Dana Rakca for men, Cipta Bakti for women

## What we want

Asrama Dana Rakca becomes the dorm for men, and Asrama Cipta Bakti the dorm
for women. This is the reverse of the rule used so far. Every screen,
choice and message that depends on who may live in a dorm follows the new
rule.

## Who it is for

Pengelola Asrama and Kepala Unit, who place residents and kakak pendamping.
Students, who see their dorm.

## What should happen

- When Pengelola Asrama adds a man as a resident, then the dorm choices are
  Asrama Wismantara Putra and Asrama Dana Rakca.
- When Pengelola Asrama adds a woman as a resident, then the dorm choices
  are Asrama Wismantara Putri and Asrama Cipta Bakti.
- When a woman is saved into Dana Rakca anyway, then the save is refused
  with "Asrama Dana Rakca hanya untuk mahasiswa laki-laki. Pilih asrama
  lain.", and the same for a man in Cipta Bakti, with "perempuan".
- When Kepala Unit makes a woman kakak pendamping of Dana Rakca, or a man
  of Cipta Bakti, then it is refused with the matching message.
- When anyone opens the Asrama summary, then Dana Rakca says "mahasiswa
  laki-laki" and Cipta Bakti says "mahasiswa perempuan".
- When the preview is filled with sample data, then every sample resident
  and kakak pendamping of Dana Rakca is a man, and of Cipta Bakti a woman,
  with their rooms and activities kept.

## Not included

- No change to dorm names, their order on screen, rooms or Wismantara.
- Records already saved are not moved to another dorm by the app. After
  merging, the owner rebuilds the main preview once from the hosting page,
  so its sample data follows the new rule.
