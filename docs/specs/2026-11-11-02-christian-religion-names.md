# Kristen Protestan and Kristen Katolik

## What we want

The Agama choices "Kristen" and "Katolik" are renamed "Kristen Protestan"
and "Kristen Katolik", on every screen that shows Agama and in
"Unggah berkas". Students already saved keep their Agama and now show the
new name.

## Who it is for

Staff who add or change students, and anyone who opens a student's
detail.

## What should happen

- When staff open Agama in "Formulir" or "Ubah", then the choices are
  Islam, Kristen Protestan, Kristen Katolik, Hindu, Buddha and Konghucu.
- When a sample student saved as Kristen is opened in "Ubah" or the
  student detail, then it shows Kristen Protestan; one saved as Katolik
  shows Kristen Katolik.
- When the list "Mahasiswa yang ditambahkan" is opened, then each row
  shows the new names.
- When staff read the "Unggah berkas" guidance, then it names Kristen
  Protestan and Kristen Katolik.
- When a file row has "Kristen Protestan" or "Kristen Katolik", in any mix
  of upper and lower case, then the row is taken with that Agama and the
  preview shows the new name.
- When a file row has only "Kristen" or "Katolik", then the row is skipped
  as an unknown Agama, and the reason names the text.

## Not included

- No other Agama is renamed, added or removed.
- No change to the saved data.

## Open questions

None.
