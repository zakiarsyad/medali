# Dorm words that differ from the campus ones

Depends on: Asrama: Dorm attendance clarification, Asrama: Dorm violations,
Asrama: Dorm dashboard, Tugas saya: My tasks: clarifications and cleaning
assessments
Group: Asrama

## What we want

Dorm attendance and campus attendance, and dorm violations and discipline
penalties, should use different words, so that someone with both pages,
such as Kepala Unit, Direktur or Ketua Program Studi, does not confuse
them. Three names are the same on both sides today:

- "Klarifikasi kehadiran" is the name of the Asrama tab, of a Kegiatan
  karakter tab, and of a panel on Kegiatan PPKS. Tugas saya calls its dorm
  sections "Klarifikasi kehadiran" and "Klarifikasi kehadiran menunggu
  validasi".
- "Kehadiran kegiatan" is the dorm attendance panel on Dashboard asrama.
  It is also the campus part of the character grade, "Kehadiran kegiatan
  (25%)", on Penilaian karakter and the student detail.
- The Asrama tab for dorm violations is called just "Pelanggaran".
  Pembinaan's "Pelanggaran disiplin" uses the same word for a discipline
  penalty.

The other Asrama tabs already say "asrama": "Kegiatan asrama", "Perizinan
asrama", "Pembersihan asrama". The resident's own number already says
"Kehadiran kegiatan asrama", and the prototype's role descriptions say
"pelanggaran asrama".

## Who it is for

- Everyone who sees Asrama, Tugas saya or Dashboard asrama, and above all
  the roles that also see the campus pages.

## What should happen

- When anyone sees the Asrama tab for dorm clarifications, then it is
  called "Klarifikasi kehadiran asrama", in the tab row and the page title.
- When anyone sees the Asrama tab for dorm violations, then it is called
  "Pelanggaran asrama", in the tab row and the page title.
- When Pengelola Asrama looks at Tugas saya, then its dorm clarification
  sections are called "Klarifikasi kehadiran asrama" and "Klarifikasi
  kehadiran asrama menunggu validasi".
- When anyone looks at Dashboard asrama, then the dorm attendance panel is
  called "Kehadiran kegiatan asrama".
- When anyone looks at the campus pages, then nothing changes:
  "Klarifikasi kehadiran" on Kegiatan, "Kehadiran kegiatan" in the
  character grade, and "Pelanggaran disiplin" on Pembinaan keep their
  words.
- When it is seen on a phone, then the longer names fit the tab row and
  the headings with no sideways scroll and no cut text.

## Not included

- Words inside a tab, such as buttons, column titles and messages. Inside
  an Asrama page they are already clear from the page.
- Any change on the campus side.

## Better than the prototype

- The prototype names these tabs without "asrama". The longer names match
  the other Asrama tabs and the prototype's own role descriptions.

## Decided

1. Only the dorm side changes. The campus names are older and used by more
   roles.
2. Only names that are seen beside the campus pages change: tabs, page
   titles, Tugas saya sections and dashboard panels.
3. "Klarifikasi kehadiran asrama" on Tugas saya is the number card at the
   top, which jumps to the section "Klarifikasi kehadiran asrama menunggu
   validasi".
4. Where a dorm screen names an Asrama tab, it uses the tab's new name,
   "Klarifikasi kehadiran asrama" or "Pelanggaran asrama", as it already
   does for "Perizinan asrama" and "Pembersihan asrama". Otherwise a link
   would name a tab that no longer exists by that name. These are, on Tugas
   saya, "Lihat semua di …" and the link after "advokasi pelanggaran
   menunggu keputusan BAM" under "Perlu dipantau"; on Klarifikasi kehadiran
   asrama, "di tab …" and "Buka tab …" in "Klarifikasi ditolak dan
   penetapan SP"; and on Kegiatan asrama, the resident's "Lihat di menu …".
5. The resident's own page of the violations tab is titled "Pelanggaran
   asrama" too, as the staff page is: it is the same tab.
6. On a phone the Asrama tab row keeps scrolling within itself, as every
   tab row with many tabs already does; each tab name stays whole on one
   line, and the page itself has no sideways scroll.
