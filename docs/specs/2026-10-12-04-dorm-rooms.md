# Dorm residents and rooms

## What we want

The app knows which students live in the dorm and in which of the four:
Asrama Wismantara Putra, Asrama Wismantara Putri, Asrama Dana Rakca or
Asrama Cipta Bakti. The Asrama menu shows only for students who live
there. The tab Ploting kamar gets its content: staff list the rooms of
each dorm per floor and put residents into them, so every later Asrama
feature knows who lives where.

## Who it is for

- Change: Pengelola Asrama and BAM - Kerumahtanggaan.
- Read only: Kepala Unit, Direktur / Wakil Direktur, Ketua Program Studi
  (their study program only) and BAM - BPHK.
- Mahasiswa who live in the dorm, who see their own room.

## What should happen

- When a student is active and either in semester 1 or 2 on the Reguler
  jalur, or in the Internasional or Pembibitan kategori in any semester,
  then they are a dorm resident. Internasional and Pembibitan men live
  in Wismantara Putra and the women in Wismantara Putri. Other women
  live in Dana Rakca, other men in Cipta Bakti. The semester is the one
  the academic period gives. Confirmed by a check.
- When a resident stops being one (Cuti, Non Aktif, or semester 3 on
  Reguler), then they leave their room and the place is free on the
  next load. Confirmed by a check.
- When the owner picks a resident in Ganti peran, then the menu shows
  Asrama and the page header shows their room, for example "Asrama
  Wismantara Putri, lantai 2, kamar 204", or "Belum diplot". When they
  pick a student who does not live in the dorm, then Asrama is not in
  the menu and typing its address is refused.
- When Pengelola Asrama opens Ploting kamar, then they see a switch
  between the four dorms, the numbers Penghuni, "Sudah diplot" and
  "Kamar terdaftar" with the total kapasitas, chips Lantai 1 to Lantai 8
  with the number of rooms, the room cards of the chosen floor with
  their residents and places taken, and the list "Belum diplot". Each
  dorm has its own rooms, floors and list.
- When they fill Nomor kamar and Kapasitas (1 to 12, 4 at the start) and
  press "Tambah kamar di lantai (n)", then the room appears. A number
  already used in that dorm is refused with a message.
- When they choose a resident in a room card, then the resident moves
  into the room and leaves "Belum diplot". Only residents of that dorm
  who have no room are offered, and a full room offers no one.
- When they press "Isi otomatis" on a room, then its free places are
  filled with residents who have no room, in NIM order.
- When they press "Keluarkan" on a resident and confirm, then the
  resident goes back to "Belum diplot".
- When they press "Ubah nomor/kapasitas", then they can change both; a
  kapasitas below the number of residents is refused.
- When they press "Hapus kamar" on an empty room and confirm, then the
  room leaves the list. A room with residents cannot be removed.
- When a read-only role opens the tab, then they see the same screen
  without any button, and the note "Menu asrama dikelola oleh Pengelola
  Asrama. Anda dapat melihat rekap dan rinciannya." Ketua Program Studi
  sees only residents of their study program.
- When Mahasiswa, Pengasuh or BAM - KI types the address of Ploting
  kamar, then it is refused. When a read-only role sends a hand-made
  room change, then the server refuses it. Confirmed by a check.
- When two staff fill the last free place of a room at the same time,
  then one is saved and the other sees "Kamar sudah penuh". A resident
  is in at most one room and a room never holds more than its kapasitas,
  kept by the database. Confirmed by a check.
- When staff open Ploting kamar on a phone, then the room cards stack in
  one column with no sideways scroll, and it shows within about 1 second
  on the preview. "Belum diplot" shows at most 60 names with "dan (n)
  lainnya" and a search.
- When the sample data command runs, then each of the four dorms has
  rooms on a few floors with some residents placed.

## Not included

- Kakak pendamping in rooms and their number.
- The Asrama column and the Asrama filter on the student lists.
- Other Asrama tabs, and their wording that names the dorms.
- The resident rule, lantai 1 to 8 and kapasitas 1 to 12 are the
  prototype's values, to be confirmed by the campus before launch.

## Better than the prototype

- A resident without a room shows as "Belum diplot" everywhere, instead
  of an invented automatic room.
- Taking a resident out of a room asks for confirmation.
- Empty states for a floor without rooms and a dorm without residents.
- Two staff cannot overfill a room at the same time.
- "Belum diplot" gets a search, so a name past the first 60 can be found.

## Open questions

None. Settled with the owner: Wismantara is two buildings, Wismantara
Putra and Wismantara Putri, plotted into rooms like the other two, so a
room never mixes men and women. A student who stops being a resident
leaves their room automatically.
