# SKPM data

Depends on: Mentor groups, Academic period
Group: Penilaian

## What we want

Penilaian gets the tab "Data SKPM". Staff upload the activity points of
the Sistem Kredit Prestasi Mahasiswa from a CSV file, one row per
activity. Each student's points are added up and turned into a Nilai SKPM
from 0 to 100, which later becomes one part of the Nilai karakter.

## Who it is for

- Upload, remove an upload, set the conversion: Kepala Unit and Penilai
  Mahasiswa (right "Unggah data SKPM").
- Read: Direktur / Wakil Direktur, Ketua Program Studi (own program),
  Pengasuh (own mentees).

## What should happen

- When a staff role opens Data SKPM, then they see four numbers
  (Mahasiswa dengan data SKPM, Rerata poin, Rerata nilai SKPM, Sumber
  data) and "Nilai SKPM per mahasiswa".
- When anyone looks at a Nilai SKPM, then it is total points ÷ "Poin
  untuk nilai 100" × 100, at most 100, to one decimal. With 50: points 8
  + 12 + 10 = 30 give 60; 17 give 34; 65 give 100. With 40: 30 give 75.
  Confirmed by a check.
- When Kepala Unit or Penilai Mahasiswa saves "Poin untuk nilai 100" (a
  whole number 1–1000, 50 at the start) with "Simpan konversi", then every
  Nilai SKPM changes and the note says "Ditetapkan (date) oleh (name)."
  Empty or outside the range saves nothing: "Isi jumlah poin yang valid."
- When they open "Unggah data SKPM", then they choose "Tambahkan" or
  "Ganti semua data", and see the columns (nim, kegiatan, kategori,
  tingkat, peran, tanggal, poin), the note that tingkat, peran and tanggal
  may be empty, "Unduh templat CSV" and a place to pick or drop a file.
- When they pick a file, then nothing is saved yet: they see how many rows
  are ready, the first six, and "Lihat baris yang dilewati" with each
  row's number and reason: "NIM tidak ada dalam data mahasiswa",
  "Mahasiswa tidak aktif", "Nama kegiatan kosong" or "Poin harus angka
  0–1000". An empty kategori becomes "Lainnya". One student may have many
  rows.
- When they press "Simpan (n) baris", then the rows are saved as one line
  in "Riwayat unggahan" (time, file, rows, students, mode, who), and the
  upload records the academic period it was made in. With
  "Ganti semua data" a confirmation comes first, and earlier uploads are
  marked "tergantikan" and no longer count, whatever period they are
  from. Points run on from one semester to the next.
- When they press "Hapus" on an upload and confirm, then it is marked
  removed, not erased, and the values are worked out without it.
- When they look at the list, then each student shows Kegiatan, Total
  poin, Nilai SKPM, with "Rincian" for the activities, search, the filters
  "Ada data SKPM" and "Belum ada data", 25 a page. A student with no rows
  shows "Belum ada data".
- When a read-only role opens the tab, then it says who uploads and how
  many points equal 100. When Pengasuh or a student sends a file or a
  conversion by hand, or Pengasuh asks for another group's student, then
  it is refused. Confirmed by a check.
- When "Simpan" is pressed twice, then the rows are saved once; the whole
  file is saved or none of it. Uploads, removals and conversion changes go
  into the log that cannot be edited. Confirmed by a check.
- When the file is not CSV, is empty, lacks a column, has more than 1,000
  rows or is larger than 1 MB, then the message says what is wrong.
- When used on a phone, then lists become cards with no sideways scroll.
  The tab shows within about 1 second on the preview.

## Not included

- Excel files. Students entering their own SKPM activities.
- The SKPM weight in Nilai karakter (Character grade list).
- Starting the points again each semester, and closing a semester.
- Refusing a kategori or tingkat outside the lists: the prototype keeps
  what the file says.
- The numbers here are the prototype's values, to be confirmed by the
  campus before launch.

## Better than the prototype

- "Ganti semua data" asks for confirmation, and applies to the whole
  file; the prototype applied it only to the first 400 rows.
- A removed upload shows who removed it and when.

## Decided

1. An upload records the academic period it was made in; points do not
   start again each semester, the same answer as for SIKU.
2. "Poin untuk nilai 100" starts at 50; Kepala Unit or Penilai Mahasiswa
   changes it with "Simpan konversi".
3. A kategori or tingkat outside the fixed lists is not refused, as in
   the prototype; the text in the file is kept.
4. The running period is read from "Academic period", only to record it
   on each upload.

## Decisions made while building

- The tab is read by the roles that hold it: Kepala Unit and Penilai
  Mahasiswa see every student, Direktur / Wakil Direktur every student,
  Ketua Program Studi their own program, Pengasuh their own mentees. No
  other role holds it, and a student does not.
- An upload keeps its file name, its mode, the academic period it was
  made in, how many rows and students it holds, who saved it and when,
  and a fingerprint of the file, never the file itself.
- Which uploads count is worked out each time, as in the prototype: those
  not removed, from the latest "Ganti semua data" that is not removed
  onward. So removing a "Ganti semua data" upload made by mistake brings
  back the uploads it had replaced.
- Poin may hold decimals, as in the prototype, kept to two places.
- Tanggal is kept as the file writes it, as in the prototype, which takes
  any text there; an empty one is shown as "-".
- Each "Simpan konversi" is kept as a line of its own with the number
  before it, so the latest is the one in force and the lines are the log.
- The sample data holds an upload replaced by a later "Ganti semua
  data", one added after it, and one removed, and leaves some Aktif
  students without data.
- The numbers and the list hold the Aktif students only, as in the
  prototype. Rerata poin and Rerata nilai SKPM are over the students with
  data, to one decimal; with none they show "-".
- "Sumber data" counts the uploads that count now; with none it says
  "Belum ada", "belum ada unggahan". The prototype's sample data for
  students without an upload is not built.
- The list puts the most points first and the students without data
  last, then goes by name. The prototype's column "Sumber" is left out,
  since every row comes from an upload.
- "Rincian" opens over the tab and lists Kegiatan, Kategori, Tingkat,
  Peran, Tanggal and Poin, the latest upload first. For a student the
  account may not see, nothing opens.
- "Konversi poin ke nilai" says "Nilai SKPM = total poin ÷ (n) × 100",
  without the prototype's "semester", since points run on. Until anyone
  sets the number it says "Angka awal, sesuaikan dengan pedoman SKPM.",
  as the prototype does; the date is written short, as on other screens.
  Under it are the kategori and tingkat of the prototype. A saved number
  says "Konversi SKPM disimpan: (n) poin = nilai 100."
- The columns are read by their titles in the first row, or in the order
  of the template when the first row is a student. As in the prototype,
  a file with titles must name all seven columns; "kegiatan" may also be
  titled "nama_kegiatan", and "poin" "skor" or "kredit".
- Poin is a number from 0 to 1000 and may have decimals after a point or
  a comma, kept to two places, as in the prototype.
- Tanggal is never checked and never skips a row, as in the prototype:
  any text is kept as written, at most 30 characters.
- Every text is kept on one line; Kegiatan is cut at 200 characters, and
  Kategori, Tingkat and Peran at 100, so a very long cell does not skip
  its row.
- The reasons for a skipped row end with a full stop, as in SIKU file
  upload. A file with too many rows or with no row says "baris kegiatan
  SKPM" in its message.
- A removed student counts as a NIM that is not in the data, as in SIKU
  file upload.
- "Unggah data SKPM" sits beside "Konversi poin ke nilai", as in the
  prototype, and starts on "Tambahkan". The note for "Ganti semua data"
  says it replaces the earlier data "dari semester mana pun", not only
  that of the running semester as the prototype says, since that is what
  it does.
- "Ganti semua data" asks "Ganti semua data SKPM? Semua unggahan
  sebelumnya ditandai tergantikan dan tidak dihitung lagi." before it
  saves; "Hapus" asks the prototype's "Hapus unggahan (file)? Nilai SKPM
  dihitung ulang tanpa data ini."
- The preview shows nim with the name under it, then kegiatan, kategori,
  tingkat, peran, tanggal and poin of the first six ready rows, and at
  most 30 skipped rows, as SIKU file upload does. On a phone the rows
  become cards. A saved file says "(n) baris dari (file) tersimpan.", as
  in the prototype.
- "Simpan" pressed twice for the same picked file is answered as the
  first press. The same key sent with another file, another mode, or by
  another account is refused and saves nothing. A file saved again on
  purpose is not warned about: the spec asks for no such warning.
- "Riwayat unggahan" is shown to every role that reads the tab, 20 a
  page, the latest first, with the counts of the whole file; only
  Kepala Unit and Penilai Mahasiswa see "Hapus". A removed upload stays
  in it with "Dihapus (time) oleh (name)". The panel shows "Belum ada
  unggahan SKPM." when there is none.
- Saving refuses while no academic period runs, as SIKU file upload
  does.
- The sample data also gives two students of another program some rows,
  so a Ketua Program Studi of that program sees data.
