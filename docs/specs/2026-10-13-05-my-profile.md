# My profile

Depends on: Student list and detail, Academic period

## What we want

A student opens Profil saya and sees their own record as the campus holds
it: who they are, where they are in their studies and their status. It is
read only. The cards about scores, discipline, health and the survey join
this page in later specs.

## Who it is for

- Mahasiswa: their own record only.
- Nobody else. Staff see students on Mahasiswa, not here.

## What should happen

- When a student opens Profil saya, then they see their name as the page
  title, then NIM, program studi and "Semester (n), tahap (name)", and
  the status Aktif, Cuti or Non aktif.
- When they look at the first card, then it shows Angkatan with jalur,
  Provinsi (domisili sesuai NIK), Semester and tahap with its level,
  Kelas ("Belum tercatat" when empty), Jenis kelamin, the category tags
  (Afirmasi, Pembibitan, Internasional, Blended) and, for alih program,
  Instansi asal.
- When the student is on Cuti or Non aktif, then the line under the name
  says "Tidak aktif" instead of the semester.
- When a student signs in, then Profil saya is the page they land on.
- When a person holds Mahasiswa and another role, for example a BAM
  role, then Profil saya shows their own student record and the menu
  also holds the other role's pages.
- When a student looks for a way to change a field, then there is none,
  and a line says "Data ini berasal dari data kampus. Bila ada yang
  keliru, hubungi Unit Pembangunan Karakter."
- When a staff account without the Mahasiswa role types the page's
  address, then it is refused.
- When a student asks for another student's profile by a hand-made
  request, then the server answers with their own record or refuses; it
  never returns another student's. Confirmed by a check.
- When a hand-made request tries to change any field of the record, then
  it is refused. Confirmed by a check.
- When a student whose record was removed (in Pengguna baru mahasiswa)
  tries to open the page, then it is refused.
- When the page is opened on a phone, then the card is one column with
  no sideways scroll.
- When the preview opens the page, then it shows within about 1 second.

## Not included

- The Pengasuh and Asrama lines (My mentor and dorm room on my
  profile).
- Every card about scores, discipline, health, notes and the survey,
  and a discipline sanction beside the name (later Profil saya specs).
- A photo, contact details and anything the student can change.

## Better than the prototype

- The page says where the data comes from and who to tell when it is
  wrong. The prototype shows the record with no way to report a mistake.
- The category tags show here as they do in the student detail.
- The page never takes the student's number from the address, so one
  student cannot ask for another's page.

## Decided

1. No photo, phone number, e-mail or emergency contact on the profile,
   and nothing a student may change. It is new personal data, and it
   waits until the app runs on the campus server.
2. Staff get no Profil saya. "Masuk sebagai" already shows who they are.

Decided while building, because the issue did not say:

3. Reading one's own student record is a named right of its own in the
   list of what each role may do, held by Mahasiswa only. It opens the
   record of the signed-in account and no other.
4. An account that holds the Mahasiswa role but has no student record,
   or whose record was removed, is refused the page.
5. The tahap name in the line under the name is in small letters, as the
   prototype writes it: "Semester 3, tahap penumbuhan".
6. While no academic period runs, no semester is known: the line under
   the name of an Aktif student shows NIM and program studi only, and
   the card shows "-" for "Semester · tahap", as the student detail
   does.
7. In the card, "Semester · tahap" follows the student detail: a student
   on Cuti still sees their semester there, and a Non aktif student sees
   "Tidak aktif". Only the line under the name says "Tidak aktif" for
   both.
8. The status and the category tags use the same words as the student
   detail: "Non Aktif" and "Blended learning".
9. "Instansi asal" shows for every alih program student, with "-" when
   none is kept, as in the student detail.
10. A student with no provinsi kept sees "-" there, as in the student
    detail.
