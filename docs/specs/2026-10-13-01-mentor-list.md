# Mentor list

Depends on: Mentor groups

## What we want

The Pengasuh menu gets the tab "Pengasuh": every mentor with their
status, and a page per mentor with the groups they held and the
students they look after now. A mentor is "Mengasuh" while holding a
group, "Cadangan" when free to take one, and "Tidak aktif" when no longer
available.

## Who it is for

- Kepala Unit, Direktur / Wakil Direktur and Pegawai UPK: every mentor.
- Ketua Program Studi: only mentors holding a group in their program.

## What should happen

- When one of the four roles opens the tab, then they see "Cari nama
  pengasuh", "Semua status" (Mengasuh, Cadangan, Tidak aktif), "Semua
  unit", and a list: name with gelar and unit, status (with the reason
  when Tidak aktif), groups, number of students. Mengasuh first, then
  Cadangan, then Tidak aktif, each by name. Below: "(n) pengasuh."
- When they press a mentor, then the mentor page shows "Semua pengasuh",
  the name, gelar and unit, a line saying which groups they hold (or
  "Pengasuh cadangan, belum memegang kelompok." or "Tidak aktif sebagai
  pengasuh sejak (date) ((reason))."), the status, "Riwayat penugasan"
  (each group, from and to, "Saat ini"), and "Mahasiswa binaan".
- When a staff account gets the Pengasuh role in Pengguna dan peran, then
  it shows here as Cadangan.
- When an account that holds no group loses the role or is removed,
  then it shows as Tidak aktif with "Akun dihapus atau peran diganti".
  For an account that still holds a group, "Mentor groups" refuses
  both.
- When Kepala Unit opens the tab, then a line says new mentors are added
  in Pengaturan > Pengguna dan peran, with a link.
- When Ketua Program Studi opens a mentor outside their program by
  address, then it is refused.
- When Pengasuh or Mahasiswa type the address, or send a hand-made
  request, then it is refused. Confirmed by a check.
- When a mentor page is opened on a phone, then there is no sideways
  scroll and the lists become cards.
- When the preview opens the tab, then it shows within about 1 second;
  the list is paged at 50 when it grows past that.

No case was met on `main` before this spec: the tab was empty. All of it
is built here.

## Not included

- Rerata and "Evaluasi terakhir" columns (Rerata comes with "Scores on
  the mentor group and mentor pages"; evaluation with "Mentor
  evaluation").
- The rerata chart on the mentor page (the same spec as Rerata).
- Marking a replaced mentor Tidak aktif with a reason such as Mutasi or
  Pensiun (it comes with "Mentor replacement").

## Better than the prototype

- No separate "Tambah pengasuh cadangan" form: a mentor is a staff
  account, so there is one place to add people and their roles.
- The list is paged when it grows.

## Decided

1. The list and the mentor page do not show the NIP. The account keeps
   its NIP as today.
2. Kepala Unit gets no "Tambah pengasuh cadangan" here. The link to
   Pengguna dan peran is enough.

Decided while building, because the issue did not say:

3. A mentor is a staff account that has the Pengasuh role, or had it
   once. One that holds a group today is Mengasuh. One that has the role
   and holds no group is Cadangan. One that lost the role, or was
   removed, is Tidak aktif. Nothing new is stored: the status is worked
   out from the groups, the account and the log of role changes.
4. "Sejak (date)" of a Tidak aktif mentor is the day the role was taken
   or the account removed, read from the log of role changes. The reason
   is always "Akun dihapus atau peran diganti" for now.
5. An account that gets the Pengasuh role back shows as Cadangan again.
6. Ketua Program Studi sees a mentor only while that mentor holds a group
   of their program today. For them the groups, the number of students,
   the history and "Mahasiswa binaan" hold only what belongs to their
   program. A mentor outside that gets the app's usual "Akses ditolak"
   page. A Ketua Program Studi with no study program sees no mentor.
7. The number of students counts the Aktif members of the groups the
   mentor holds today, as the member count of a group does. "Mahasiswa
   binaan" lists them by name with NIM, group, kelas akademik and
   semester, and those on Cuti with a "Cuti" tag.
8. A student in "Mahasiswa binaan" opens the student detail in a box
   over the mentor page for Kepala Unit, Direktur / Wakil
   Direktur and Ketua Program Studi. For Pegawai UPK a student is not a
   link, and a hand-made request for the detail is refused, as on the
   group page.
9. Each group in the list and in "Riwayat penugasan" is a link to the
   group's page. "Riwayat penugasan" shows the newest first, and "Belum
   pernah memegang kelompok." for a mentor without any. A mentor without
   students today sees "Tidak ada mahasiswa binaan saat ini."
10. "Semua unit" offers only the units of the mentors the account may
    see. A mentor without a unit shows the gelar alone.
11. With no match the list says "Tidak ada pengasuh pada filter ini."
12. Seeing the mentors is its own right in the list of what each role may
    do, "Melihat daftar pengasuh", held by the four roles above.
13. Dates are written short, such as "1 Sep 2025", as on the other
    screens.
